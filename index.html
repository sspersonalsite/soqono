— What We Do

We operate at the intersection of engineering, product, and ops. We don't write the model, design the chip, or build the robot. We run the programs that bridge your team's ambitions and real-world results. Zero-to-one builds. Research to production. Hard programs with no existing template. We own the work start to finish.

001 Field

Custom Field Execution

Hardware data collection, prototype evaluation, structured user studies, longitudinal cohorts. We write the protocol, recruit the participants, run the sites, and ship the deliverable. Whatever form that takes.

002 Program

Embedded PMs & Ops Staff

Execution bandwidth or full program leadership, we embed either. PMs, ops leads, and field staff who have already shipped this kind of work. We can execute a plan you've built, or design and own the whole program ourselves.

Frontier-Tech Programs

Autonomous vehicles. Robotics. Multimodal AI. Pre-release hardware. NDA-grade environments with proven information security practices. We've been here before.

— Where We Work

Global Scale

US-based leadership on every program. For domestic work, all staff are US-based W-2 employees. For international programs, we deploy our own staff onsite and collaborate with a trusted bench of vetted global partners, without fragmenting your point of contact. One vendor, regardless of where the work runs.

Lab & Field Spaces

We work where the program requires. Client sites, hotels, venues, private residences, outdoor environments, a ski lodge if that's what it takes. For programs that benefit from a more traditional setup, we also have access to configurable lab spaces in New York and Los Angeles. We source it, configure it, and run it.
