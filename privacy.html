001

Information Collected

Soqóno collects personal information from you, which may include:

  • Name

Information Collected Automatically

Information about your computer hardware and software is automatically collected by Soqóno when you use the Site. This may include your IP address, browser type, operating system type, device type, language preferences, access times and dates, geographic location, referring website addresses, and other technical information. We may also collect information about how you interact with the Site. This information is used for our business purposes, including operation and improvement of the Site, technical troubleshooting, quality maintenance, and general statistics.

Cookies and Other Information Collection Tools

A cookie is a small file sent to your computer when you visit a website. We may use "session cookies" (temporary, expire when you close your browser) and "persistent cookies" (remain on your device until you or your browser erase them).

The Site may use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies — necessary for you to browse the Site and use its features.
  • Functional Cookies — remember choices you have made and improve how the Site works for you.
  • Analytics Cookies — collect information about how you use the Site to help us understand visitor interactions.
  • Marketing Cookies — track your online activity to help deliver relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness.

We use cookies to improve the Site, analyze traffic, and detect fraud. We may use tools such as Google Analytics to evaluate visitor use of the Site. If you would like to opt out from Google Analytics, you may use Google Analytics' opt-out browser add-on. To learn more about managing and deleting cookies, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

Web Beacons and Pixels

We may use pixels — HTML code snippets embedded in the Site or email messages — to collect information about user behaviors and interactions. These tools may be used to measure the success of marketing campaigns, compile Site usage statistics, and track user activities.
